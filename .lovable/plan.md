# Lead with Purpose — One-Page Book Landing Page

## Build
- Replace the placeholder homepage with a polished, single-page editorial experience for Daniel Carter’s book.
- Use the uploaded book cover in the hero and book-detail sections, and the uploaded Daniel Carter portrait in the author section without altering either image.
- Build the requested sequence: minimal sticky navigation, hero, purpose pillars, book details, editorial quote, author profile, reader-fit statements, final call to action, and compact footer.
- Make every navigation and call-to-action link scroll smoothly to its relevant section.

## Visual direction
- Translate the cover’s charcoal, ivory, amber-gold, muted slate, and restrained forest-green palette into reusable site colors.
- Pair elegant Cormorant Garamond display typography with Manrope for readable body copy.
- Use subtle texture, fine rules, sunrise/path motifs, restrained shadows, and gentle scroll reveals—without gradients, glass effects, bulky cards, or excessive motion.
- Preserve strong hierarchy and generous editorial spacing across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Technical details
- Keep the page lightweight and static in React with semantic sections and small reusable presentational components.
- Store the supplied images as optimized hosted project assets and reference those originals in the page.
- Add page-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Verify the final experience at desktop and mobile widths, including navigation, image proportions, overflow, and reduced-motion behavior.
